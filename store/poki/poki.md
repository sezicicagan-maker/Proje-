# Poki gönderimi: Reactor Buddy

**Oynanabilir bağlantı:** https://sezicicagan-maker.github.io/Proje-/reactor/

## Oyun adı
Reactor Buddy: Power the City

## Kısa açıklama (EN)
Help Buddy, a cute little reactor, light up the city! Slide the power lever to give the houses just as much electricity as they need, and splash water when Buddy gets too hot. Night falls, the big match starts, a toy factory opens: every moment the city wants something different. Earn stars, unlock 12 levels and dress Buddy up with crowns, wizard hats and cool glasses. Grown-up players can switch to the Expert Simulator with realistic RBMK, PWR, BWR and CANDU reactors.

## Nasıl oynanır (EN)
- Slide the lever: right = more power, left = less.
- Keep the white arrow inside the green zone on the house meter.
- Tap the water button when Buddy turns red and sweaty.
- Finish the level to earn stars and buy hats.

## Kontroller
Dokunmatik ve fare: kol ve su düğmesi. Uzman simülatörde klavye: ↑↓ güç, ←→ debi, M otomatik, X basılı tut: kapatma, Boşluk: duraklat.
