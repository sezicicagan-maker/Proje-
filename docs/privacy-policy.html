Evolute – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Evolute is an offline game. This version does not require an account and does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What is stored

Game progress (currency, upgrades, collection, settings such as language and sound) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Erase progress" in Settings deletes it.

Third parties

This version contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code. If a future version adds advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before release and the Google Play "Data safety" section will be updated accordingly.

Children

Evolute is not directed at children under 13.

Son güncelleme: 2026-10-28

Oyun ilerlemesi (para birimi, yükseltmeler, katalog, dil ve ses gibi ayarlar) yalnızca cihazında, uygulamanın yerel depolamasında tutulur ve cihazından çıkmaz. Uygulamayı kaldırmak veya Ayarlar'daki "İlerlemeyi sil" bunları siler.
